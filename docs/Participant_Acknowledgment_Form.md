## About This Session

You are invited to try a virtual reality application as part of a study. You will wear a Meta Quest 2 headset and complete a few short tasks using hand tracking. After each task or group of tasks, you will rate how demanding it felt using a short questionnaire.

## Health and Safety

For more detail, you may refer to Meta's full health and safety warnings: [https://www.meta.com/legal/quest/health-and-safety-warnings/](https://www.meta.com/legal/quest/health-and-safety-warnings/)

VR can cause motion sickness, dizziness, eye strain, or discomfort in some people. In rare cases, flashing lights or patterns may trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy.

## Equipment Care

The headset and computer belong to the Viper Lab. During the session, please:

- Handle the headset carefully and follow the test administrator's instructions.
- Stay inside the marked testing area.
- Tell the test administrator right away if anything seems damaged or isn't working.

## Voluntary Participation

Participation is voluntary. You may take a break or stop at any time for any reason without needing to explain why.

After the session, we recommend resting especially if you are sensitive to motion sickness.

## Data Collected

During the session, we record:

- Task results: whether each task was completed, time taken, and number of attempts.
- Your questionnaire ratings.
- Basic background information: age range, prior VR experience, etc.

Data is stored under your participant ID only. Your name is not stored with study data and results are reported only in anonymous form.
